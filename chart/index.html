      The Serpent Bearer

      Ophiuchus, the thirteenth constellation

      Ophiuchus is the Serpent Bearer. The Greeks saw Asclepius in these stars, the healer so gifted he could bring the dead back to life. It is the wisest point in the sky: higher knowing, higher healing, the kind of understanding that sees the whole of a thing at once. It lies on the Sun's path between Scorpio and Sagittarius, and the twelve-sign zodiac your chart uses leaves it out. The coral arc on your wheel marks where it sits.
