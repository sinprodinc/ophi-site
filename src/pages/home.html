Our namesake

Why Ophiuchus?

Ophiuchus [ohfeeYOUkiss] is known as the Ser­pent Bearer. It is the for­got­ten, hid­den thir­teenth con­stel­la­tion of the zo­di­ac, po­si­tioned between Scor­pio and Sagit­tar­ius.

It sits along the Sun's path, yet it was left out­side the tra­di­tion­al twelve-sign sys­tem most of us were taught to follow. This constellation is the one as­so­ci­at­ed with ul­ti­mate heal­ing, deep know­ing, and higher wis­dom.

Ophi­uchus asks us to re­mem­ber that there is always more to the story: more nu­ance, more mys­tery, more truth bub­bling be­neath the sur­face. That is why Ophi­uchus, or Ophi, became our chosen brand rep­re­sen­ta­tion.

Like a mirrorball, Astrology reflects every angle of our souls: the patterns, the perspectives, the shadows... Ophi's work is rooted in the belief that self-knowl­edge can become col­lec­tive heal­ing, and healing is where the story begins.

Ophiuchus asks us to remember:

There is always more to the story.
