Find the element on your tin. Keep a pen and a scrap of paper close before you light it.

Fire MoonSet This B*tch On Fire

The Fire ritual

For Aries, Leo and Sagittarius Moons. Poured with Jupiter in Leo, and the fireplace on. Light to reignite your creative flame.

  1. Before burning, take a seat and take a breath.
  2. Write down what angers you. Exhale.
  3. Write down what motivates you. Exhale.
  4. Light your candle and let the flame take care of the cleanse.
  5. Take a few more breaths.
  6. Create.

The Air ritual

For Gemini, Libra and Aquarius Moons. Poured with the Sun in Libra, reciting positive affirmations. Light to create head space.

  1. Before burning, take a seat and take a breath.
  2. Write down your intrusive thoughts.

  6. Flow.

Candle careNever leave it burning unattended. Set on a heat-safe surface. The tin gets hot. Do not burn more than 3 hours at a time. Let the tin cool before closing the lid. Ingredients: Soy wax, fragrance oil, wick, magic.
