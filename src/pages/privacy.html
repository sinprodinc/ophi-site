Your privacy at Ophi by Bri

Ophi by Bri offers astrology readings by Bri Godard. A reading asks for personal details, starting with when and where you were born, so this page explains plainly what is collected, why, who helps handle it and what you can ask for. Ophi by Bri is operated by Sinprod Entertainment, based in British Columbia, Canada.

What we collect

When you book a reading: your name, email address, date, time and place of birth, anything you add in the booking notes, and your booking time. If you buy a gift reading, we collect the same birth details for the person receiving it, which you confirm you have their permission to share.

When you pay: payments are handled by Stripe. We see that you paid, the amount and the last four digits of your card. We never see or store your full card number.

During your reading: sessions are recorded by Ophi by Bri, and may be transcribed and summarised with Wispr, a note-taking tool, to prepare your recording, transcript and takeaways. By booking, you agree to the session being recorded for this purpose. Clients may not record sessions themselves.

When you email or message us: whatever you choose to include, such as a testimonial.

When you join the mailing list: your first name, email address, rising sign and the date you agreed to receive emails.

When you use the free chart: nothing. The chart on ophibybri.com/chart/ is calculated entirely in your own browser. The birth details you type there are not sent to us or stored.

When you visit the site: our host, Cloudflare, keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the site. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a calculation library from jsDelivr, which receive your IP address when they send those files. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not currently use analytics.

Why we use it

  • To prepare, give and follow up on your reading, and to deliver your recording and takeaways.
  • To manage bookings, payments, cancellations and rescheduling.
  • To send the monthly email, only if you have opted in. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep accurate business and tax records, as the law requires.

We never sell your information, and we never share your birth details, readings or recordings publicly. Testimonials are only shared with your permission.

Who helps us handle it

We use a few trusted services to run the business. Each only receives what it needs for its job:

  • Cal.com for booking and scheduling.
  • Stripe for payments.
  • Google Workspace (Meet, Drive and Gmail) for sessions, email and delivering your materials.
  • Wispr for session notes and transcripts.
  • Kit for the mailing list.
  • Cloudflare for hosting the website.

Some of these services store information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

Where your reading lives

Your recording, transcript and takeaways are delivered to a private Google Drive folder that only your email address can open.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and payment records for as long as Canadian tax law requires (generally six years). [Recordings, transcripts and session notes are kept for X, then deleted.] You can ask us to delete your reading materials sooner, unless we need to keep a record by law. Mailing list details are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Ask us to correct anything that's wrong.
  • Withdraw your consent, for example by unsubscribing or asking us to delete your materials.

Email ophibybri@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Access to client information is limited to Bri, and reading materials are shared only with the client's own email address.

Questions or concerns

Contact us at ophibybri@gmail.com or [mailing address]. If we can't resolve a concern, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
