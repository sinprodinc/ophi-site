FireMoonSet This B*tch On Fire

Aries✦Leo✦Sagittarius

The Fire ritual

For when your self-expression, frustration, or anger needs a physical outlet.

Fire Moons aren't necessarily meant to sit still with every feeling. Sometimes frustration builds because there's something you want to express, change, or finally do something about. Movement can help release some of that pressure, whether it's dancing, going for a run, or getting started on a project you've been putting off. Your restlessness might simply be asking to be put to good use.

Poured with Jupiter in Leo, and the fireplace on. Light to reignite your creative flame.

The scent

Clove✦Juniper✦Vanilla

Clove opens warm and spicy, with an earthy bite that wakes you up. Juniper keeps it bright and clean, like cold air beside a fire. Vanilla settles underneath and keeps the heat steady.

Six steps✦in order

  1. Step1

    Get it moving.

    Before lighting your candle, put on a song that makes you feel something. Stretch, dance, shake out your arms, or go for a quick walk. Whatever feels good. This isn't about getting in a workout. It's about giving yourself a physical outlet instead of sitting there getting increasingly annoyed.

  2. Step2

    Let's point out what's bothering you.

    Grab a piece of paper and write down what's been frustrating you lately. Is there something you haven't said? Something you've wanted to do but keep putting off? Don't worry about finding a solution just yet. Start by being specific about what's getting under your skin.

  3. Step3

    Figure out what you want to do about it.

    Take another look at what you've written. Is there something you can change? A conversation you need to have? An idea you'd like to pursue? Write down one thing that would help you feel excited, motivated, or a little more like yourself.

  4. Step4

    Light your candle.

    Take a moment to watch the flame. It moves, shifts, and burns steadily when it's cared for. Think about how you could give your own energy that same sense of direction, without throwing yourself into everything at once.

  5. Step5

    Make your move.

    Choose one small action you can take toward what you want. Send the message, start the project, make the plan, or finally give that idea a chance. It doesn't have to be a dramatic declaration. Starting somewhere is enough.

  6. Step6

    Don't burn yourself out.

    You don't have to solve everything or do it all today. You've figured out what needs attention and taken a step toward addressing it. Give yourself credit for that, and know when it's time to stop.

Your reflection

Where has my frustration been building, and what could I do to give that energy a healthier outlet?

ARIES MOON✦YOUR PERSONAL TAKEAWAY

Say it before it boils.

Aries Moons may feel things fast and want to act on them just as fast. That speed is honest, and the feeling often passes once it has somewhere to go. The trouble starts when you sit on it. If something's been simmering, give it an outlet before you give it a speech: walk around the block, then say the thing in one clear sentence. You don't need the whole argument prepared to be heard.

Something to think about

What have I been holding back from saying, and what's the one-sentence version of it?

LEO MOON✦YOUR PERSONAL TAKEAWAY

Clap for yourself first.

Leo Moons may feel most like themselves when their warmth and effort are noticed. Wanting to be seen isn't vanity. It's how you refuel. But when the people around you are distracted, the silence can start to feel personal. Before you go looking for reassurance, write down three things you did this week that you're quietly proud of. Then tell one person about one of them. Asking for a little applause is allowed.

Something to think about

What have I done lately that deserves recognition, and who could I let see it?

SAGITTARIUS MOON✦YOUR PERSONAL TAKEAWAY

Stay for the second half of the feeling.

Sagittarius Moons may handle a heavy mood by looking for the exit: a plan, a joke, a trip, a change of scenery. Perspective is one of your gifts, and laughing at something can genuinely shrink it. Just notice when moving on happens a little too quickly. Before you book the next thing, give whatever's bothering you ten honest minutes. The adventure will still be there, and you'll pack lighter.

Something to think about

Is there a feeling I've been trying to outrun, and what might it tell me if I let it catch up?

EarthMoonCalm The F*ck Down

Taurus✦Virgo✦Capricorn

The Earth ritual

For when you've been taking care of everything except yourself.

Earth Moons often feel more comfortable when life has some structure, familiar routines, and a few things they can depend on. But there's a difference between feeling grounded and feeling responsible for absolutely everything. When the to-do list never seems to end, even taking a break can start to feel like another task. This ritual is about slowing down, noticing what you need, and remembering that you don't have to earn a little comfort.

Poured during a Capricorn Moon, in silence. Light to root yourself in your own body.

The scent

Cardamom✦Iris✦Leather

Cardamom is warm and a little sweet, a spice that slows you down. Iris is soft and powdery, the quiet in the middle. Leather gives it weight, something solid to lean on.

Six steps✦in order

  1. Step1

    Get comfortable.

    Find somewhere you can relax without being interrupted. Change into something soft, grab a blanket, and put your feet on the floor. Notice the feeling of being supported by something solid. For the next little while, you don't need to be doing anything else.

  2. Step2

    Take stock of what you've been carrying.

    Write down three things that have been taking up your time or energy lately. Maybe it's work, family responsibilities, expectations you've put on yourself, or something you've been worrying about. Whatever comes to mind.

  3. Step3

    Let's see what can wait.

    Look at your list. Is there something that doesn't actually need to be done today? Something that could be simplified or shared with someone else? Circle one thing you can take off your plate, even temporarily.

  4. Step4

    Light your candle.

    Pay attention to the scent, the warmth, and what's happening around you. Notice the ground beneath your feet, the chair supporting your body, and the sounds in the room. You don't have to meditate perfectly. Just give yourself a moment without trying to accomplish something.

  5. Step5

    Do something that feels good.

    Make a cup of tea, enjoy a snack, wrap yourself in a blanket, or settle into your favourite spot. Whatever feels comforting. And here's the important part: try doing it without checking your phone, answering emails, or mentally reorganizing tomorrow's schedule.

  6. Step6

    Leave something unfinished.

    Yes, really. Choose one nonessential task that can wait until tomorrow. The world probably won't fall apart because you didn't get to it today. Your body and mind deserve some attention, too.

Your reflection

What would help me feel genuinely supported right now, and what responsibility could I put down to make room for it?

TAURUS MOON✦YOUR PERSONAL TAKEAWAY

Check what your comforts are comforting.

Taurus Moons may feel most settled with good food, a familiar routine, and their own bed. Those comforts are real medicine, and you don't owe anyone an apology for wanting them. Change can feel jarring when it shows up faster than you'd like. Tonight, pick one comfort that genuinely restores you and give it your full attention. Then notice whether anything you're holding on to is there out of habit rather than because it still feels good.

Something to think about

What am I holding on to because it's familiar, and is it still taking care of me?

VIRGO MOON✦YOUR PERSONAL TAKEAWAY

Let good enough be good.

Virgo Moons may cope by fixing: wiping the counter, making the list, catching the mistake before anyone else does. That instinct makes you useful, and the people around you are lucky to have it. It can also mean your nervous system never gets a day off. Choose one thing this week that you'll leave at eighty percent, and see what happens. Usually, nothing. Sometimes, a little relief.

Something to think about

What am I putting pressure on myself to get exactly right, and what would change if I let it be good enough?

CAPRICORN MOON✦YOUR PERSONAL TAKEAWAY

Hand something over.

Capricorn Moons may deal with feelings by getting busy and handling it, then feeling it later, alone. Being the dependable one is a strength, and people trust you for a reason. But your needs don't move down the list just because you can carry a lot. Pick one task this week and give it to someone else, without redoing it after. Letting someone help is not the same as falling behind.

Something to think about

What responsibility could I share this week, and what would it take to let someone help without feeling guilty?

AirMoonGhost 'Em

Gemini✦Libra✦Aquarius

The Air ritual

For when you've been thinking about everything and getting nowhere.

Air Moons often process emotions through ideas, conversation, and trying to understand what's happening around them. That can be helpful, but when one thought turns into twenty, things can get pretty noisy upstairs. You might spend so much time thinking about how you feel that you forget to check in with the actual feeling. This ritual is about sorting through the mental clutter and figuring out what deserves your attention.

Poured with the Sun in Libra, reciting positive affirmations. Light to create head space.

The scent

Sage✦Sea Salt✦Ambrette Seed

Sage is green and herbal, the clearing note. Sea salt keeps it open and airy, like a window cracked by the ocean. Ambrette seed is soft and musky, a skin-close warmth that keeps a clear head from feeling cold.

Six steps✦in order

  1. Step1

    Disconnect for a minute.

    Put your phone on silent, close the extra tabs, and step away from whatever has been demanding your attention. The group chat can survive without you for a little while.

  2. Step2

    Get it all out of your head.

    Grab a piece of paper and write down everything you've been thinking about. Unfinished conversations, decisions, worries, random reminders, whatever. Don't worry about making it organized or even particularly readable. This is a brain dump, not an essay.

  3. Step3

    Let's sort through it.

    Look at what you've written. Which things actually need your attention? Which ones are outside your control? And which ones have you been thinking about simply because you haven't given yourself permission to stop?

    Circle what you can do something about. Cross out what doesn't need to be dealt with right now.

  4. Step4

    Light your candle.

    Take a few comfortable breaths and watch the flame for a moment. If another thought pops into your head, that's fine. You don't have to chase after it or figure it out immediately. It'll still be there if it's important.

  5. Step5

    Figure out what you actually need.

    Maybe there's a conversation you've been avoiding, a decision you keep putting off, or something you're ready to stop giving your attention to. Choose one small thing that would help you feel less mentally crowded.

  6. Step6

    Give your mind the night off.

    Fold up your paper and put it somewhere safe. If there's something important on it, you can come back to it later. For now, do something that doesn't require you to analyze, explain, or make another decision.

Your reflection

What have I been spending too much time thinking about, and is there anything I actually need to do about it?

GEMINI MOON✦YOUR PERSONAL TAKEAWAY

Turn down the noise, then listen.

Gemini Moons may process feelings by talking, texting, reading, and keeping the mind busy. A good conversation genuinely helps you, and it can untangle what a week of brooding couldn't. But a constant stream of input can drown out the quieter signals. Give yourself one hour with no phone, no podcast, and no group chat. Write down what you notice once the noise stops.

Something to think about

What might I notice about how I feel if I stepped away from my phone for an hour?

LIBRA MOON✦YOUR PERSONAL TAKEAWAY

Put your preference on the table.

Libra Moons may feel most at ease when things are fair, pleasant, and talked through. Keeping the peace is a real skill, and people feel safe around you because of it. But agreeable can quietly turn into invisible. Next time someone asks what you want, answer before you check what they'd prefer. A relationship that can handle your opinion is worth more than one that only works when you don't have one.

Something to think about

Where have I been saying yes when I'd rather say no, and what would I ask for if no one could be disappointed?

AQUARIUS MOON✦YOUR PERSONAL TAKEAWAY

Feel it before you explain it.

Aquarius Moons may step back from a feeling to understand it, almost like studying it from across the room. That distance can bring real clarity, especially when everyone else is reacting. But understanding an emotion isn't the same as letting yourself have it. Try writing down how you feel without justifying it. No because, no context, no making it sound reasonable. Just the feeling.

Something to think about

What would I say about how I'm feeling if I didn't have to make it sound reasonable?

WaterMoonCry Bby xo

Cancer✦Scorpio✦Pisces

The Water ritual

For when you've been feeling a lot and haven't had a chance to sort through it.

Water Moons are often sensitive to emotional changes, whether those feelings come from their own experiences or the people around them. Sometimes an argument, a memory, or even someone else's mood can stick around longer than expected. And when you're used to caring about how everyone else is doing, your own feelings can end up taking a back seat. This ritual is about checking in with yourself and figuring out what you need, without feeling responsible for everybody else's emotional weather.

Poured during a Uranus X Moon Trine. Light to release emotional blockages.

The scent

Rose✦Geranium✦Amber

Rose is soft and tender, the heart of it. Geranium leans green and fresh and keeps the rose light. Amber is warm and resinous underneath, like being held while you let it all out.

Six steps✦in order

  1. Step1

    Find somewhere you can be yourself.

    Choose somewhere private and comfortable. Grab a glass of water, your journal, and anything else that makes you feel relaxed. Maybe that's a favourite blanket, some music, or simply closing the door for a little while.

  2. Step2

    Check in with how you're feeling.

    Before you try to make sense of anything, ask yourself how you've actually been feeling lately. Sad? Irritated? Overwhelmed? Maybe you're happy and haven't given yourself much time to enjoy that either. Write down whatever comes up without worrying about whether it makes sense.

  3. Step3

    Let's figure out what's behind it.

    Look at what you've written. Is there something that happened recently? Someone you're worried about? A feeling you've been trying to push aside? Think about what might help. Maybe you need reassurance, a little space, or a conversation you've been avoiding.

  4. Step4

    Light your candle.

    Take a moment with the scent and warmth of your candle. You don't need to force an emotional breakthrough. If you feel like crying, that's okay. If you don't, that's okay too. The point is to give yourself a chance to feel whatever is there.

  5. Step5

    Let the water mark a fresh start.

    Take a slow sip of water, or wash your hands with comfortably warm water away from the candle. Use the moment to think about what you've been carrying. Is there something you can do about it? Or is it something you need to acknowledge without taking responsibility for fixing?

  6. Step6

    Do one thing for yourself.

    Maybe you need some quiet, a good conversation, an evening without anyone asking you for something, or a little reassurance from someone you trust. Pick one thing that would genuinely help, and give yourself permission to ask for it or make it happen.

Your reflection

What feelings have I been carrying lately, and how much of that is actually mine to take care of?

A little something for your Moon

CANCER MOON✦YOUR PERSONAL TAKEAWAY

Let someone take care of you for once.

Cancer Moons may feel safest at home, nesting, or looking after someone they love. You're often the first to notice when someone needs comfort, and the first to offer it. That generosity can leave your own reserves running low, especially when an old hurt is still hanging around. This week, let someone return the favour. Accept the offer, say yes to the dinner, or ask directly for the thing you'd normally be giving.

Something to think about

Who makes me feel cared for, and how could I let them show up for me this week?

SCORPIO MOON✦YOUR PERSONAL TAKEAWAY

You don't have to keep it all inside.

Scorpio Moons may prefer to work through difficult feelings privately, especially when trust is involved. There's nothing wrong with being selective about who gets access to your inner world. But if something has been weighing on you, consider sharing a little of it with someone who's earned your trust. You don't have to tell the whole story to stop carrying it entirely on your own.

Something to think about

Is there something I've been keeping to myself that's starting to feel heavier than it needs to?

PISCES MOON✦YOUR PERSONAL TAKEAWAY

Hand back what isn't yours.

Pisces Moons may pick up the mood of a room before anyone says a word. That sensitivity makes you compassionate and deeply intuitive, and it can also leave you carrying feelings that started with someone else. Music, water, and quiet can help you settle back into yourself. After your ritual, ask of each heavy feeling: did this start with me? If it didn't, you're allowed to set it down.

Something to think about

Whose emotions have been taking up space in my day, and what do I need when I set them aside?
