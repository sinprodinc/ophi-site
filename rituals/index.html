FireMoonSet This B*tch On Fire

The Fire ritual

For Aries, Leo and Sagittarius Moons. Poured with Jupiter in Leo, and the fireplace on. Light to reignite your creative flame.

  1. Before burning, take a seat and take a breath.
  2. Write down what angers you. Exhale.
  3. Write down what motivates you. Exhale.
  4. Light your candle and let the flame take care of the cleanse.
  5. Take a few more breaths.
  6. Create.

The scent

Clove✦Juniper✦Vanilla

Clove opens warm and spicy, with an earthy bite that wakes you up. Juniper keeps it bright and clean, like cold air beside a fire. Vanilla settles underneath and keeps the heat steady.

The scent

Cardamom✦Iris✦Leather

Cardamom is warm and a little sweet, a spice that slows you down. Iris is soft and powdery, the quiet in the middle. Leather gives it weight, something solid to lean on.

AirMoonGhost 'Em

The Air ritual

For Gemini, Libra and Aquarius Moons. Poured with the Sun in Libra, reciting positive affirmations. Light to create head space.

  1. Before burning, take a seat and take a breath.
  2. Write down your intrusive thoughts.
  3. Sit in silence for a few moments, then release an audible exhale.
  4. Light your candle and let the flame take care of the cleanse.
  5. Take a few more breaths and quiet the mind for a few seconds.
  6. Flow.

The scent

Sage✦Sea Salt✦Ambrette Seed

Sage is green and herbal, the clearing note. Sea salt keeps it open and airy, like a window cracked by the ocean. Ambrette seed is soft and musky, a skin-close warmth that keeps a clear head from feeling cold.

The scent

Rose✦Geranium✦Amber

Rose is soft and tender, the heart of it. Geranium leans green and fresh and keeps the rose light. Amber is warm and resinous underneath, like being held while you let it all out.

Candle careNever leave it burning unattended. Set on a heat-safe surface. The tin gets hot. Do not burn more than 3 hours at a time. Let the tin cool before closing the lid. Ingredients: Soy wax, fragrance oil, wick, magic.
