The story

The Journey Towards Reclaiming Our Collective Power

October is asking some uncomfortable questions. Who holds the power? What is the power being used for? What are we willing to tolerate? And… How much of ourselves have we compromised?

On October 3rd, Venus stations retrograde in Scorpio, beginning a period of reflection on our relationships, values, desires, feminine resources, and sense of worth. Venus is traditionally associated with love, beauty, pleasure, money, and connection. Scorpio brings our attention to what lives beneath the surface, ie: vulnerability, intimacy, trust, control, and the complicated ways we share power… Or are supposed to.

This isn't necessarily comfortable territory.

On the same day, Mars in Leo forms an exact opposition to Pluto in Aquarius, bringing another layer of intensity to the conversation. Mars symbolizes our drive, courage, anger, and capacity to act. Pluto is associated with power, transformation, and the forces that shape our lives, both personally and collectively.

Together, these transits offer a potent symbol of the struggle to reclaim our voices and our agency, particularly when we feel unheard, restricted, or disconnected from our own authority (did someone say feminism?).

There is also a powerful invitation here to examine our relationship with feminine power: how it has been celebrated, controlled, silenced, or oppressed. Not that feminine power belongs to any one gender, but receptivity, desire, intuition, and the right to determine what happens to our bodies and lives deserve the same recognition and respect as celebrities, sports, male-led podcasts, politics, and the stock market (to name a few).

What's going on

Venus retrograde in Scorpio, Mars opposes Pluto, and the courage to question out loud.

First We Observe, Then We Readjust.

Retrogrades are traditionally understood as periods of revisiting, reconsidering, and reassessing. Venus retrograde asks us to look closely at what we value and whether our choices reflect those values.

The first part of this process may be about observation. Notice what feels out of balance. Where are you giving more than you receive? Where have you surrendered control that should belong to you? What are you no longer accepting?

As the month unfolds, reflection may begin to turn into readjustment.

A final thought for October

October's astrology invites us to get curious — deeply curious. It invites us to recognize what needs to change without demanding instant resolution, as real change happens over time. Remember that our individual power grows stronger when it's supported by care, connection, and accountability.

What feeds your internal power? What drains it?
