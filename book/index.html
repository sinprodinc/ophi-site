FAQ

Which reading should I book?+

Start with Meet Yourself if you've never had your chart read. It's the full foundation: your Sun, Moon and Rising, your chart ruler, your major aspects and the patterns that tie them together.

Book Plot Twist when you're navigating a major change, like a Saturn return, a breakup, a job loss or a career change, and want your chart and current transits read through that lens.

Book a Quickie when you want a short, focused look at a few placements.

I don't know my birth time. Now what?+

Check your birth certificate first, or call the hospital where you were born. Either one can usually give you the exact time, and an exact time always leads to a more detailed reading. A parent or relative may know it too.

If none of that works, book anyway. We'll leave out the parts that depend on the time and cover everything else, which is still a full, meaningful reading. We may even work out your rising together during the session.

What do I need to send you?+

Your date of birth, your time of birth (as exact as you can find it) and the city and country you were born in. You'll enter them when you book. Booking a Plot Twist? Add the topic you'd like to focus on in the booking notes.

How does the session work?+

Every reading is one-on-one and virtual. Pick a time on the booking page and you'll receive a link to join by email. Meet Yourself is a 50-minute reading plus 10 minutes for your questions. Plot Twist is 45 minutes plus 5. The Quickie is 25 minutes plus 5. Come as you are, with any questions you'd like to bring.

Can I record it?+

Please don't record the session yourself. I record every reading and send you the full recording afterward, so you can listen back without taking notes and your reading is handled with care.

What are the digital takeaways?+

Every reading comes with the full session recording and personalized takeaways.

Meet Yourself also includes a full transcript of your reading and curated digital copies of the major highlights in your chart. Plot Twist includes highlights on the topics we discussed. The Quickie includes a one-page PDF overview of the placements we covered.

The deeper the reading, the more you get to keep. Everything arrives in a private folder that only your email can open.

Will you tell me what's going to happen?+

No. Astrology shows patterns, timing and tendencies, not a fixed script. I'll talk about what a placement or a transit may bring up for you and how you can work with it. What you do with it is always yours.

Is this therapy?+

No. A reading is reflective, spiritual guidance. It can sit alongside therapy, but it isn't a substitute for therapy or for medical advice. If you're going through something serious, please reach out to a qualified professional as well.

What happens to my birth details?+

They're used only to prepare and give your reading, and they're never shared or posted. Your reading materials go into a private folder that only your email can open.

The free chart on this site works entirely in your browser: the birth details you type there are not sent to me or stored. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Can I gift a reading?+

Yes. When you buy a gift reading, I'll need the birth details of the person receiving it: their date, time and place of birth.

Not sure of their birth time? Ask in a low-key way: cast your own chart on the chart page and compare charts with each other. If they don't know it, their birth certificate or the hospital they were born in can usually tell them. If the time can't be found, the reading still goes ahead and we cover everything we can.

Cancelling or rescheduling

Rescheduling is free up to 24 hours before your reading.

Cancelling more than 24 hours before your reading? You choose. If you're not planning to book with Ophi for a while, you'll get a full refund. If you'd like to rebook within the next 3 months, your reading stays credited to your account so your next booking is processed quicker. A credit that isn't used within 3 months expires.

Cancelling with less than 24 hours' notice? Ophi by Bri may keep 50% of the payment.
