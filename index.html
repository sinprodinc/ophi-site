Our namesake

Why Ophi?

Ophi is short for Ophiuchus (pronounced oh-fee-YOO-kus), the often-overlooked thirteenth constellation along the Sun's path through the sky.

Known as the Serpent Bearer, it's long been associated with the symbolism of healing, wisdom, and miracle transformation.

While it doesn't belong to astrology's traditional twelve zodiac signs, I like what it represents: there's always more to the story than what we first see. And sometimes, the most interesting discoveries come from what's been overlooked for too long.
