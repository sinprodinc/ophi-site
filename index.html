Why Ophiuchus?

The Thirteenth Constellation

Ophiuchus [ohfeeYOUkiss] is known as the Ser­pent Bearer. It is the for­got­ten, hid­den thir­teenth con­stel­la­tion of the zo­di­ac, po­si­tioned between Scor­pio and Sagit­tar­ius.

It sits along the Sun's path, yet it was left out­side the tra­di­tion­al twelve-sign sys­tem most of us were taught to follow.

That feels mean­ing­ful.

The one left in the shad­ows is the one as­so­ci­at­ed with ul­ti­mate heal­ing, deep know­ing, and higher wis­dom.

Ophi­uchus asks us to re­mem­ber that there is always more to the story: more nu­ance, more mys­tery, more truth bub­bling be­neath the sur­face.

That is why Ophi­uchus, or Ophi, became our chosen brand rep­re­sen­ta­tion.

To me, as­trol­o­gy is a mir­ror­ball. It re­flects every angle of some­one's soul with­out praise or judg­ment. Only pat­terns and per­spec­tive. Ophi's work is rooted in the belief that self-knowl­edge can become col­lec­tive heal­ing, and that the inner force we carry is not some­thing to chase, or prove.

It is a unique sig­na­ture that al­ready be­longs to us.
