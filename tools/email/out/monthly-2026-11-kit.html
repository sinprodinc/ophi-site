The collective reading
A moment of clarity before the next adventure.

November picks up where October left off: somewhere between a revelation, a reckoning, and long-overdue data review. With Mercury retrograde in Scorpio and Venus retrograde in Libra, we're revisiting the uncomfortable stuff (again)... Power dynamics, buried truths, unbalanced relationship dynamics, and the ways we've learned to keep the peace, often at our own expense.

On November 4th, Mercury meets the Sun in Scorpio in a stellar moment called cazimi, offering a revealing flash of insight into what we've been trying to understand the last few months. A Scorpio New Moon on November 8th invites us to begin working with those discoveries through holding space. Then, on November 13th, both Mercury and Venus station direct. The wheels begin turning forward again, though not at full speed.

Some realizations need a little time to settle.

Mid-month, Mars joins Jupiter near the South Node in Leo, bringing courage, creative ambition, and a healthy dose of dramatic flair. But there's a catch: not every battle is a war, and not every victory requires celebration. The challenge is to act from conviction rather than ego.

What’s the purpose, y’knowwhatimsayin’?

We’re gettin’ weird, and we’re so ready for it.

By November 24th, a Full Moon in Gemini sets the stage for a gorgeous Uranus x Pluto trine on November 29th. New perspectives, thrilling conversations, and challenges to familiar systems may open possibilities we hadn't considered before.

Collectively, these transits invite us to question who controls information (I mean, really… think about it), whose voices are heard, whose are silenced, and how new technology and community can contribute to meaningful change.

 
A final thought for November

Understanding what needs to change is only half the battle. Channeling the courage, creativity, and community to do something about it?

That's where our collective story gets interesting.
