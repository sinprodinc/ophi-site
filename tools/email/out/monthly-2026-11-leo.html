November 2026 ✦ Leo Rising
Redefining home and adapting to a new spotlight.

November begins close to home. Your family, living situation, emotional roots, and sense of belonging have been undergoing some serious reconstruction. Mercury's cazimi in Scorpio may help you understand what needs to change or begin an important conversation you've been avoiding, even if that conversation is with yourself. Venus turning direct in Libra also encourages you to revisit your communication habits and the POV’s you've subscribed to. Sometimes changing the dialogue changes the foundation.

Then comes your moment in the spotlight. Mars and Jupiter meet in Leo mid-month, bringing a surge of courage, ambition, and creative energy. You're allowed to take up space, but with the South Node involved, it's worth asking whether you're acting from genuine desire or a need for recognition. The Gemini Full Moon later brings developments within friendships and communities. As Uranus connects with Pluto in your relationship sector, new social connections or changing group dynamics may help you rethink what partnership looks like. You can shine just as bright in a community as you do by yourself.
